# Homerun wiring status

Audit date: **2026-09-13**, contract-status rows refreshed **2026-09-19**. This records source-level wiring, with an additional read of the installed SDK deployment registry. Post-launch project management and current Operator profiles are implemented. Frontend deployment status must be checked independently from source-level wiring. A working handler is not proof of a completed production transaction. No production-chain transactions were submitted for this audit.

## Current Owner and Operator dataflow

1. Published metadata supplies introductions, images and profile addresses. [CurrentOperatorProfile](../src/components/CurrentOperatorProfile.tsx) uses the published Operator as a **planned** recipient before a verified INCOME connection exists.
2. Once INCOME exists, [the current Operator reader](../src/lib/project-operator-profile.ts) verifies the registered revnet contracts and reads the active stage's reserved-token split recipients from one canonical block. Project routes, hook recipients, zero-address defaults and burns are not presented as Operator wallets.
3. Both [FUND](../src/components/FundProject.tsx) and [INCOME](../src/components/IncomeProject.tsx) overviews now use this reader. They show current direct recipients, including multiple recipients when configured, and withhold current-address claims when verification fails. A profile's name/photo/introduction is shown only when its published wallet matches the current recipient. Owner profiles similarly follow verified FUND ownership without assigning an old Owner's introduction to a replacement wallet.
4. [Operator replacement](../src/components/IncomeOperatorActions.tsx), [general split edits](../src/components/ProjectSplitsEditor.tsx) and other [admin transactions](../src/hooks/useProjectAdminTx.ts) invalidate the relevant reads after confirmation. Confirmed-block tracking prevents an older RPC response from being presented as the updated Operator.

## Intentional boundaries

- **Founder Haus and local previews:** `/founderhaus` and `/project` use local simulation state. Phase selection, modeled activity, payments, inventory and the new details/control/permissions/split editors do not submit transactions. See [ProjectPage](../src/components/ProjectPage.tsx), [DemoProjectShop](../src/components/DemoProjectShop.tsx), and [demo management](../src/components/DemoProjectManagement.tsx).
- **Published plans versus enforced rules:** Saving a setup field to `projectUri` does not enforce it. Minimum revenue/consequences, forecasts, asset budgets and planned allocations are metadata. Initial FUND creation installs the fundraising rules; later lifecycle changes require separate authorized transactions. See [metadata](../src/lib/fund-project-metadata.ts), [initial FUND rules](../src/lib/fund-contracts.ts), and [create review](../src/components/CreateFlow.tsx).
- **Management scope:** Metadata edits do not transfer control or change economic splits. The split editor divides the configured reserved amount or payout group; it does not change the reserved-token rate, issuance schedule or withdrawal budgets, and cannot shorten existing onchain locks. Permission edits affect one project, not inherited global grants; a standard INCOME control wallet cannot grant powers its REVOwner contract does not authorize. These boundaries are enforced by the [metadata](../src/lib/project-metadata-edit.ts), [split](../src/lib/project-splits-edit.ts) and [authority](../src/lib/project-authority.ts) adapters.
- **Offchain operations:** Asset purchases, sales and offchain contribution settlement remain external human operations. Onchain allowance, withdrawal, treasury-return, mint and refund paths exist. Their presence does not verify a purchase, recover externally held money, or automate the stated revenue consequences.
- **Safe execution:** A proposed Safe transaction still requires execution through Safe. The app tracks confirmation; a proposal is not reported as completed execution.

## Validation and audit method

The management implementation passed the full Vitest suite, model tests, lint, the indexer registry check, TypeScript and a production build in an isolated checkout. The contract suite passed 132 tests as of 2026-09-19. Browser checks covered demo management at 1440, 390 and 320 pixels, plus live read-only FUND/INCOME management at desktop and mobile widths, with no serious or critical accessibility violations. These checks do not establish successful production wallet execution or deployment of the add-on contracts. See [release preparation](INCOME_RELEASE.md).

The wiring audit traced visible handlers through transaction builders and checked their integration into the project pages. The 2026-09-19 refresh rechecked the installed `@bananapus/nana-sdk-core` registry through `jbContractAddress['6']`: `HomerunDeployer` and `HomerunAllowlistHook` remain absent on all eight supported chains.
