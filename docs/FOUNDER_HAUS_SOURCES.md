# Founder Haus: public source audit

Reviewed **9 September 2026** for the Homerun demo. This audit records public descriptions and image provenance; it does not verify an investment, asset ownership, operating accounts or a valuation. The demo's purchase budget, reserve, income, expenses and token terms remain hypothetical scenario inputs.

## Venue facts suitable for the demo

| Detail | Evidence and permitted interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Name and location | The [official homepage](https://founderhaus.club/) identifies **Founder Haus Beach Club** in **Jurerê Internacional, Florianópolis, Brazil**. Use this neighborhood and city; an exact street address is not established here. |
| What it is | The [official homepage](https://founderhaus.club/) presents a members' venue for founders, with company off-sites, meetups and other business events. It describes the club as part of the larger Founder Village. |
| Work and accommodation | The [membership page](https://founderhaus.club/membership) advertises workspaces, wellness activities, community events and accommodation access in some plans. This establishes advertised services, not occupancy, room inventory or rental receipts. |
| Pool and shared spaces | The [club's host role](https://founderhaus.club/work-with-us/host) explicitly describes a pool, bar, lounges and member spaces. These are official operational descriptions, not a physical inspection. |
| Suites and offices | The [housekeeping role](https://founderhaus.club/work-with-us/housekeeping-supervisor) refers to suites, offices, restrooms and event areas. It gives no room count or measured floor area. |
| Wellness programming | [Closing Week](https://founderhaus.club/closing-week) advertises coworking and wellness sessions including breathwork, sauna and cold plunges. Treat these as program offerings, not proof that every advertised activity occurs in the photographed building. |

Suggested short description: **“A founders' clubhouse in Jurerê Internacional, Florianópolis, with workspaces, events and a pool.”** The rooftop photograph can be captioned as an official Founder Haus image showing a covered roof terrace and an ocean view. Avoid implying that the image proves title, boundaries or uninterrupted beach access.

## Keep the venue separate from the wider organization

The homepage explicitly distinguishes Founder Haus from the broader Founder Village and links to [foundervillage.xyz](https://foundervillage.xyz/). That external site did not yield readable content during this audit, so no additional facts depend on it.

[Founder Desk](https://founderhaus.club/founderdesk) separately advertises office and business-support services. [AIA](https://founderhaus.club/aia) describes planned computing infrastructure and is marked as forthcoming. Neither establishes additional facilities owned by this house.

The official application bundle also contains separate `/clubhaus1`, `/clubhaus2` and `/clubhaus3` listing routes with different house descriptions. Their relationship to the current Beach Club is not established in this audit. Do not transfer their room counts, addresses, capacities or photographs to the demo asset.

## Gaps that remain open

- No verified asset price, appraisal, historical revenue, expenses, occupancy or bank statements were obtained. Published membership prices and community statistics are not evidence of collected income.
- No title record, parcel identifier, beneficial owner, lease, mortgage or other lien evidence was reviewed. Public references to community ownership or revenue sharing describe an offer; they do not establish the demo investor's legal rights.
- No exact building area, bedroom count, guest capacity or current condition was verified for the pictured venue.
- A registered business address or corporate stated capital would not establish the house address, property ownership or purchase price. Neither is used here.

The official site renders through JavaScript. Facts above were checked against indexed versions of the named official pages and their publicly served application text. They are dated public claims, not independent attestations, and should be refreshed before publication as offering information.
